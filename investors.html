THE INVESTMENT CASE

Why Invest in Vaijanti?

Sovereign-Backed Revenue

Our PPAs are signed with state Discoms, NTPC, and SECI — government-backed offtakers with 25-year tenures, providing predictable, inflation-linked cash flows.

Rajasthan's Solar Advantage

Plants located in India's highest GHI zones — Jaisalmer, Barmer, Bikaner — consistently deliver above-P90 generation, maximising yield on every rupee invested.

Proven O&M Track Record

Our in-house operations team has maintained above-98% plant availability across all projects since commissioning — a key driver of investor returns.

SEBI Registered & Audited

Fully compliant with SEBI, CERC, and RERC regulations. Annual audits by Big Four firms. All project SPVs are structured for clean, bankable investment.

Growing Pipeline

550+ MW under development — Barmer 300 MW, Nagaur 150 MW, Jalore 100 MW — providing a clear path to doubling operational capacity by 2027.

ESG & Green Credentials

ISO 14001 certified. All projects generate RECs (Renewable Energy Certificates). Ideal for ESG-mandated funds, green bonds, and sustainability-linked financing.
