To be a world class leader in businesses that enrich lives and contribute to nations in building infrastructure through sustainable value creation.

UNLEASHING BOUNDLESS
POTENTIAL

Vaijanti Green Energy is a fast-growing enterprise headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, built on the belief that clean power and modern infrastructure are the foundation of a self-reliant India. Since 2019, we have grown a diversified portfolio spanning renewable energy, highways, industrial infrastructure, and real estate.

Our renewable energy business combines utility-scale solar installations with Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) production, delivering over 500 MW of operational clean power capacity. Our highways and industrial infrastructure businesses are engineered to global O&M standards for long-term reliability and national impact.

Vaijanti's growth is guided by a simple philosophy: purpose before profit. We are committed to expanding our environmental and social footprint through responsible business practices, community-focused initiatives, and a long-term commitment to sustainability, integrity, and shared prosperity.
