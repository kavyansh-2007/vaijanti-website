POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENTS

PPA Structures We Offer

Government / Discom PPA

State Discoms, NTPC, SECI

Selling solar electricity directly to state electricity distribution companies (Discoms) and central government bodies like SECI and NTPC through competitive bidding.

TERM 25 years
TARIFF Competitive Bid-based
CERC/RERC Compliant Grid Connected AAA Rated Offtakers

Industrial Open Access PPA

Factories, Mines, Cement, Textile

Direct sale of power to large industrial consumers using open access transmission. Bypass rising grid tariffs and secure fixed-rate green power.

TERM 10–25 years
TARIFF Fixed, Below Grid Tariff
Reduced Carbon Footprint Interstate/Intrastate OA ESG Compliance

Group Captive Structure

Industrial Clusters, SEZs

Industrial consumers group together to consume power from a specific solar plant, benefiting from captive consumption exemptions on electricity duty.

TERM 15–25 years
TARIFF Mutually Agreed Shared Rate
Captive Exemptions Multi-consumer Model Shared Asset Advantage

Commercial & C&I PPA

Hospitals, Hotels, Malls, Data Centers

Fixed-rate solar power for large commercial and service-oriented establishments seeking to reduce energy costs and enhance sustainability credentials.

TERM 10–20 years
TARIFF Fixed with Annual Escalation Cap
Green Energy Certification Reduced Operational Cost Predictable Overheads
